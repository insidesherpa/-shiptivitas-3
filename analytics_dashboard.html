Three Actionable Feature Ideas to Increase DAU

1. Smart Notifications & Daily Digest

Hypothesis: Users moving cards are highly engaged. Sending personalized daily digests listing updates and assigned items creates habit loops to pull team members back.
Expected Impact: +15-25% DAU
Feature: Morning notifications summarizing pending tasks, mentions, and updates, paired with real-time assignment alerts.

2. Comments & @Mentions on Cards

Hypothesis: Regression occurrences (16 events moving cards back) imply coordination gaps. Centralizing team discussions on cards reduces reliance on external tools.
Expected Impact: +20-30% DAU
Feature: In-card comment threads, tag alerts (@user), attachments, and automatic inbox notifications.

3. Streaks & Leaderboards

Hypothesis: With 51.5% completion velocity, visual feedback systems drive user gratification, boosting daily check-ins.
Expected Impact: +10-20% DAU
Feature: Streak alerts, weekly velocity widgets, badges for completed tasks, and clean team leaderboards.
