💡 Three Actionable Feature Ideas to Increase DAU

1. Smart Notification System & Daily Digest

Hypothesis: Users who are actively moving cards and making progress are more engaged. By sending personalized daily digest emails showing team activity, card movements, and pending items, we can create habit loops that bring users back daily.

Expected Impact: +15-25% DAU increase

Feature: Implement a smart notification system that sends:
• Morning digest with "cards needing attention"
• Real-time notifications when cards are assigned or moved
• Weekly progress summaries with team metrics
This leverages the proven engagement pattern (users logging in to check/update cards) and amplifies it through timely reminders.

2. Collaborative Comments & @Mentions on Cards

Hypothesis: The 16 regression events (cards moving backward) suggest communication gaps. Adding collaborative features will increase return visits as users come back to respond to discussions and check mentions.

Expected Impact: +20-30% DAU increase

Feature: Add a comment thread to each card with:
• @mention functionality to tag team members
• File/image attachment support
• Activity feed showing all card updates
• Email notifications for mentions
This creates "pull factors" that bring users back to respond, similar to social media notification patterns.

3. Gamification: Progress Streaks & Team Leaderboards

Hypothesis: With 51.5% of cards completing the full workflow journey, users are motivated by completion. Adding visible progress tracking and competitive elements will increase daily check-ins.

Expected Impact: +10-20% DAU increase

Feature: Implement a gamification layer including:
• Daily login streaks with visual badges
• Team productivity leaderboards (cards completed this week)
• Personal velocity metrics ("You completed 5 cards this week, 2 more than last week!")
• Achievement badges for milestones (first card completed, 10-day streak, etc.)
This taps into intrinsic motivation and social proof to drive habitual usage.
