### Idea 1: Add card color labels
- **Hypothesis**: Users want visual priority signals.
- **Expected Impact**: Increase engagement and task organization.
- **What it is**: Allow users to assign color tags to cards (e.g. red = urgent).

### Idea 2: Daily summary email
- **Hypothesis**: Managers want easy end-of-day insight.
- **Expected Impact**: Increases usage from managers.
- **What it is**: An email listing all status changes and new cards.

### Idea 3: Search/filter bar for cards
- **Hypothesis**: Users are struggling to find specific cards.
- **Expected Impact**: Improves task retrieval speed.
- **What it is**: Add a filter bar by card name, status, or date.
